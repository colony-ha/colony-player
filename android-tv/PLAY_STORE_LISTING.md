# Google Play listing draft — COLONY PLAYER

**App name:** COLONY PLAYER – Media Player  
**Short description:** Play your own authorized M3U and Xtream playlists on Android TV and Google TV.

## Full description

COLONY PLAYER is a media player designed for Android TV and Google TV. Connect your device, add your own authorized M3U/M3U8 or Xtream playlist through the COLONY PLAYER website, and enjoy your media with a TV-friendly interface and remote-control navigation.

### Features
- Android TV and Google TV interface designed for the TV remote.
- Add and manage your own playlists through the COLONY PLAYER website.
- Live TV, movies and series sections when included in your playlist.
- Search and favorites.
- Video playback powered by Android Media3.
- Device-based access with a seven-day trial and optional annual activation.

### Important
COLONY PLAYER does not provide, sell, host, or bundle TV channels, movies, series, or streaming subscriptions. Users must provide their own playlists and have the rights or permission to access the content. Availability and playback quality depend on the user's content provider and internet connection.

## Data disclosures to verify in Play Console
The app uses a device identifier and device key to register and authenticate a TV device; the service stores a hash of the device key. It processes subscription and session information. Playlist names and playlist source URLs are stored for the user's device; playlist URLs are encrypted at rest. Limited connection metadata is processed for rate limiting and security. Streaming requests are sent to the third-party source selected by the user. The app does not include advertising or in-app analytics in this build.
